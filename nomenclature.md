# THE INFERENCE — Nomenclature

Locked-in naming for the project. All reader-facing language must conform to this. No "brief", "digest", "rundown", "summary", or generic newsletter words. When stuck, ask: what's the football/matchday equivalent?

## Brand

- **THE INFERENCE** — the masthead and product name. Dual semantics: football ("great inference by the defender to read that through ball" — anticipation, reading the game) and AI/ML (model inference, the act of generating output from a trained model).
- Each daily issue is an *Inference*, numbered across the tournament: *Inference N° 01 — Opening Match* through *Inference N° 35 — The Final*.
- The *matchday-programme metaphor* still informs the editorial structure of each issue (team sheet, stats page, centre spread, manager's notes) — but the word "Programme" no longer appears on the masthead.

## Product formats (à la carte)

| Format | Name | Referent |
|---|---|---|
| Daily personalized email / web issue | **today's Inference** | The thing itself |
| Audio companion to the daily issue | **THE TOUCHLINE** | Where the manager paces; where reporters stand |
| Pre-match podcast (before your team's match) | **WALKOUT** | The walk out of the tunnel onto the pitch |
| Post-match podcast (after your team's match) | **FULL-TIME** | The whistle that ends the match |
| Live in-match text commentary | **THE GANTRY** | Where the commentary team sits |
| Reader Q&A feature | **THE MIXED ZONE** | Where journalists interview players post-match |
| Daily visualization | **CENTRE SPREAD** | The magazine centerfold |

## Sections within today's Inference

| Section | Contents |
|---|---|
| **TEAM SHEET** | Your teams' day — stat lines, group position, what's next |
| **STATS PAGE** | Universal numbers digest — top scorers, leaders, ratings ladder |
| **CENTRE SPREAD** | The visualization (shot map / pass network / xG plot) |
| **MANAGER'S NOTES** | The AI tactical observation — short, stat-grounded |
| **FROM THE STANDS** | Reader Q&A snippet — the best question from yesterday |
| **ADDED TIME** | Closing anomalies kicker — the unexpected stuff |

## Notes

- **FULL-TIME** (post-match podcast) and **ADDED TIME** (closing section) are deliberately distinct, not synonyms. ADDED TIME means "the unexpected bit at the end, still part of the match" — fits anomalies perfectly.
- **CENTRE SPREAD** appears both as a standalone à la carte product and as a section within today's Inference. Same content, two surfaces.
- Section labels work in both the email and the audio version — THE TOUCHLINE can "read out today's TEAM SHEET" and it lands.
