Nº 07 — After The Whistle

MIXED ZONE

the bit where you get a word in ↘

In football, the mixed zone is the corridor after the final whistle where players and press actually talk. This is ours — one inbox, no switchboard, answered by the same person who staples the thing.

matchday@matchdayinference.com

Nº 01 — Good Reasons To Write

What lands well here

  • A correction. The machine gets a stat or a name wrong — tell us. We keep a hand-edited layer exactly for this.
  • A request. Want a team added, a lens tuned, a fixture covered? Say so.
  • A question about how any of it works, or about your data — see Privacy first, then ask.
  • A hello. It's a passion project. Hearing the zine landed somewhere is the whole pay.
Already subscribed and want out? Every issue has an unsubscribe link, or just email and ask. No hoops.
Nº 02 — The Honest Bit

Response time

This is one person and a stack of code, not a support desk. During the tournament the mornings belong to stapling issues, so replies may take a day or two. They do come.
